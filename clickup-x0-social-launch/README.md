# ClickUp Social Launch Concept

Five mock posts for a hypothetical ClickUp X.0 launch:

1. Pre-launch teaser option 1 — X and Instagram, with full-bleed square X (1200 × 1200) and 4:5 Instagram (1080 × 1350) versions.
2. Pre-launch teaser option 2 — LinkedIn and X, with full-bleed square X and 4:5 versions.
3. Subway billboard, option 1 — ClickUp X.0 now available.
4. Launch video — X-first, square (1200 × 1200), with a 4:5 master (1080 × 1350). It shows ClickUp 4.0 switching off, ClickUp X.0 switching on, and a UI walkthrough.
5. Product feature carousel — five click-through cards for LinkedIn and Instagram.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The carousel shows one card at a time with controls.

ClickUp X.0 and its features are hypothetical interview concepts. UI and sample content are illustrative. ClickUp branding remains the property of its owner; font licenses are included in `assets`.
